Prompt Engineering
Project Overview
Business Problem
Various organizational departments are facing specific operational challenges[cite: 2]:

Finance: Struggling to accurately forecast budgets due to unpredictable revenue streams and fluctuating expenses[cite: 2]. The department also faces difficulties in effectively detecting fraudulent transactions hidden within large volumes of complex data[cite: 2].
HR: Finding it difficult to conduct fair and consistent performance evaluations across diverse teams, leading to employee dissatisfaction[cite: 2].
Operations: Facing challenges in optimizing resource allocation and managing raw material shortages with suppliers[cite: 2].
Marketing: Struggling to build strong brand awareness and market positioning in competitive environments[cite: 2].
Company/Industry Selected
Mid-sized firms operating across various sectors, including retail, financial services, technology, manufacturing, and consumer goods[cite: 2].

AI/ML Solution Proposed
The proposed solution involves utilizing advanced Prompt Engineering strategies across Large Language Models (LLMs) to generate executive-ready deliverables[cite: 2]. This includes:

Zero-Shot Prompting (Type 1): Implementing the RCTF (Role, Context, Task, Format) framework to assign specialized domain roles and generate structured business reports[cite: 2].
Few-Shot Prompting (Type 2): Utilizing synthetic industry benchmarks (e.g., JPMorgan, Toyota, Google) as reference examples to anchor formatting consistency and deliver highly granular outputs[cite: 2].
AI Tools Used
ChatGPT (GPT-3.5): Selected for providing a reliable balance of overall performance and broad accessibility[cite: 2].
Claude (Sonnet 5 Medium): Chosen as a mid-tier model highly optimized for complex reasoning and generating structured, formatted responses[cite: 2].
Perplexity (Free Model): Included specifically to benchmark cost-effective, freely available AI solutions for practical business use[cite: 2].
Gemini (Flash Model): Selected as a lightweight model designed specifically for speed and efficiency in real-time tasks[cite: 2].
Key Prompts Used
Basic Prompt (Baseline): "My finance department is struggling in predicting the expenses and revenue steam what to do?"[cite: 2].
Improved Prompt Type 1 (RCTF): "Act as a financial analyst. Our company is a mid-sized retail firm. Our target audience is internal management and stakeholders. Create a detailed expense and revenue forecasting report using historical financial data, seasonal trends, and market growth indicators. Present the output as a structured business report with clear tables, charts, and actionable recommendations."[cite: 2].
Improved Prompt Type 2 (Few-Shot): Building upon Type 1 by adding an explicit example: "Example: Forecast revenue of JPMorgan in Q2 based on market growth and historical performance. Now, using the same style, generate a forecast for our company’s expenses and revenue streams for the upcoming financial year. Take synthetic numbers."[cite: 2].
Key Findings
Basic Prompting: Yields high-level, generic bullet points that lack role definition and are insufficient for executive decision-making[cite: 2].
Type 1 Improved Prompting: Successfully generates structured corporate reporting, framework development, and formal business deliverables with tables and KPI frameworks[cite: 2].
Type 2 Improved Prompting: Delivers highly aligned, granular outputs with standardized rating rubrics, phased rollouts, and numerical breakdowns suitable for prototyping complex business frameworks[cite: 2].
Business Recommendations
Adopt the RCTF (Role, Context, Task, Format) prompting framework across all departments to transition from exploratory brainstorming to generating actionable business reports[cite: 2].
Utilize Few-Shot prompting with synthetic benchmarks when training models on specific stylistic standards without exposing confidential company data[cite: 2].
Select the appropriate LLM based on the task requirement, such as using Claude for structured reports or Gemini for speed-dependent real-time tasks[cite: 2].
Key Learning/Reflection
Prompt Engineering requires clear and specific instructions to yield useful responses[cite: 2].
If an AI generates a poor or generic answer, iteratively improving the prompt (adding context and formatting rules) significantly enhances the output[cite: 2].
AI-generated answers must always be verified for accuracy, relevance, completeness, bias, and evidence[cite: 2].
Confidential corporate information should never be shared with public AI tools[cite: 2].
